Windows

A smoother close. A quicker return.

While enabled, WinBend must be running to handle lid-close sleep.

Windows' lid action differs from WinBend's setting. Toggle this setting off and on to apply it again.

Windows will stop handling lid-close sleep itself. WinBend will fold and then sleep the laptop. If WinBend is not running, closing the lid may leave it awake.

When enabled, anyone with physical access may be able to use your unlocked session after waking the laptop.

Allow Windows to resume without asking for your sign-in? This makes your session accessible to anyone who wakes the laptop. Windows Hello or organization policies may still apply.
